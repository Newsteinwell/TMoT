## Initial regression evidence

Five time domains, 390train/130validation/130test windows; 24-step lookback, six-step mean target, nonoverlapping raw intervals. Two seeds; prespecified hyperparameters. Lower RMSE is better. Units are Wh per ten-minute target interval.

| Method | Final RMSE mean ± sampleSD | Mean stage RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fine-tuning | 61.90 ± 0.21 | 65.78 |
| TMoT without historical loss | 63.65 ± 0.28 | 70.18 |
| TMoT | 71.20 ± 1.65 | 76.47 |
| Feature replay | 77.48 ± 1.19 | 71.80 |
| Cumulative ridge | 90.64 ± 0.00 | 89.27 |

Additional no-training references: seen-training-target mean RMSE60.69, last-value persistence65.41, last-six-input mean62.56. A raw-timestamp nonoverlap audit passed across all three splits. These results **do not show a TMoT advantage**. Exact moment arithmetic passes its tests, but the proposed adaptation/regularization does not yet demonstrate an accuracy benefit. The next diagnostic should examine target/input scaling, historical-vs-current risk and the bottleneck/readout mismatch on validation data. No hyperparameters have been changed in response to these test results. The two ridge seeds produce the same predictions, as expected for this analytic setup, and are not independent statistical evidence.
